# Visual brief

## Concept

A forbidden independent-radio transmission printed as a Polish samizdat zine.

## Intended feeling

Urgent, physical, literate, rebellious. It should feel assembled by people with something to say, not decorated by a marketing template.

## Audience and action

Polish listeners interested in Bitcoin, NOSTR, privacy, sovereignty, and independent media. The primary action is finding and playing an episode from the best surviving public source.

## Composition

- strict editorial grid under deliberate surface irregularity
- oversized compressed headlines
- black/orange/paper fields separated by hard rules
- recovered artwork as evidence, not generic decoration
- numbered catalog cards rather than rounded podcast tiles

## Palette

- dirty paper `#f0eadb`
- near-black `#11100d`
- signal orange `#ff4d00`
- faded graphite `#6a665c`

## Typography

System-only condensed display stack for headlines and a plain sans-serif for reading. Monospace appears only for archival labels and metadata.

## Motion

Only short hover shifts and transitions. No scroll choreography. Honor reduced-motion preferences.

## Avoid

Gradients, glass, neon cyberpunk, Bitcoin coin art, microphones, waveform wallpaper, rounded SaaS cards, fake metrics, excessive noise, ransom-letter clichés, and animation without meaning.
